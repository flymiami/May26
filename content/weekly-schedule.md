# The Weekly Schedule — Mini FLY Duck

The repeating 7-day skeleton. **Same structure every week, fresh content inside it.**
This is the heartbeat of the account — people learn the rhythm and come back for "their" day.

> Post **once per day, at the same time** (recommended: 6:00 PM ET). Vertical 9:16 reel.
> Cross-post the same video to TikTok + YouTube Shorts.

---

## The week at a glance

| Day | Pillar | Theme | Primary goal | Audio |
|-----|--------|-------|--------------|-------|
| **Mon** | 🕺 **Move Monday** | Dancing | Reach (broad) | Trending |
| **Tue** | 🛹 **Trick Tuesday** | Extreme sport / adrenaline | Reach + wow | Trending / hype |
| **Wed** | 🧠 **Wisdom Wednesday** | Inspiration quote + a message | Saves | Original / calm |
| **Thu** | 💬 **Real-Talk Thursday** | Advice / help someone in need | Connection | Soft original |
| **Fri** | 😂 **Funny Friday** | Joke / pun / mini-skit | Shares | Trending / comedic |
| **Sat** | 🌴 **Adventure Saturday** | Miami lifestyle / try-something / vlog | Relatability | Vibey trending |
| **Sun** | ☀️ **Sunday Reset** | Gratitude / self-care / recharge | Community | Warm original |

**Why this order works:**
- **Open strong** — Monday dance is your widest-reach format to start the week.
- **Alternate broad ↔ deep** — reach days (Mon/Tue/Fri) pull new eyes; value days
  (Wed/Thu/Sun) convert them to fans and earn saves/shares.
- **End soft** — Sunday's wholesome reset builds the emotional bond that makes Monday hit.

## Daily detail

### 🕺 Monday — "Move Monday" (Dancing)
- **What:** FLY does a short, clean dance to a trending sound. One move, looped, fun.
- **Hook (first 0.5s):** duck already mid-bounce + bold on-screen text ("Monday? We dance.").
- **Length:** 7–12s, seamless loop.
- **Caption:** "New week, new moves. Tag who's dancing with us. 🕺✌️"
- **CTA:** "Follow for a dance every Monday."

### 🛹 Tuesday — "Trick Tuesday" (Extreme sport)
- **What:** FLY tries an extreme sport — skate, surf, BMX, snowboard, parkour, dunk.
  Big air, comedic landing optional. Aspirational + funny.
- **Hook:** the drop-in / the launch. Start *at* the action.
- **Length:** 8–15s.
- **Caption:** "Send it. 🛹 Would you try this? 👀"
- **CTA:** "Follow to see what FLY sends next."

### 🧠 Wednesday — "Wisdom Wednesday" (Inspiration quote)
- **What:** One strong quote with a real message, delivered by FLY (text on screen, calm
  scene). The duck *means* it. Built to be **saved**.
- **Hook:** the quote's first 3 words, big, on a clean frame.
- **Length:** 6–10s (or a 3-slide carousel for extra saves).
- **Caption:** the full quote + "Save this for when you need it. 💛"
- **CTA:** "Follow for a midweek reminder."

### 💬 Thursday — "Real-Talk Thursday" (Advice / helping)
- **What:** FLY gives gentle big-brother advice, OR a little story of him helping someone
  in need (sharing food, cheering up a sad character, lifting someone up).
- **Hook:** a relatable problem ("Feeling behind? Real talk…").
- **Length:** 8–15s.
- **Caption:** the advice in one line + "Who needs to hear this today? Tag them."
- **CTA:** "Follow for Thursday real talk."

### 😂 Friday — "Funny Friday" (Joke / skit)
- **What:** A clean joke, groan-worthy pun, or 2-beat skit. Pure share-bait.
- **Hook:** set up the joke in the first second; land it fast.
- **Length:** 6–12s.
- **Caption:** the punchline or "😂 send this to someone who needs a laugh."
- **CTA:** "Follow for a laugh every Friday."

### 🌴 Saturday — "Adventure Saturday" (Lifestyle)
- **What:** FLY out in the "world" — Miami beach, sneaker shopping, food, a mini day-vlog,
  trying a new thing. Builds the character's universe + relatability.
- **Hook:** "Come with FLY to ___."
- **Length:** 10–20s.
- **Caption:** "Where should FLY go next? 🌴 Drop it below."
- **CTA:** "Follow the journey."

### ☀️ Sunday — "Sunday Reset" (Recharge / gratitude)
- **What:** Cozy, slow, warm. FLY relaxing, reflecting, grateful, setting the week up.
  The emotional anchor of the week.
- **Hook:** "Before the week starts…"
- **Length:** 8–14s.
- **Caption:** a small gratitude + "What are you grateful for this week? 💛"
- **CTA:** "Follow for your Sunday reset."

## Production rhythm (don't make these daily!)

**Batch on the weekend, drip all week.** Mascot accounts die when creators try to make
content live every day. Instead:

- **Sun or Mon:** produce all 7 reels for the *next* week in one session.
- **Schedule** them via Meta Business Suite (IG) + TikTok Scheduler + a tool like Later/Metricool.
- **During the week:** you only *engage* (reply to comments as the duck), not produce.

See [`production/production-playbook.md`](../production/production-playbook.md) for the
exact make-it workflow, and [`content/content-pillars.md`](content-pillars.md) for 4 weeks
of concrete ideas to fill this skeleton.

## Flex rules

- **Trend override:** if a sound/format is blowing up, hijack it for that day's pillar
  (e.g. a trending dance on Monday, a trending meme format on Friday). Trends > schedule.
- **Holidays/events:** swap the nearest matching pillar (e.g. a "skate" Tuesday becomes a
  themed one). Keep the cadence, bend the content.
- **Series within pillars:** turn a pillar into a numbered series ("FLY tries every sport,
  Day 4") — series drive follows because people want the next episode.
