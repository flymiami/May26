# PILOT — "Move Monday: The FLY Pull-Up"

Turnkey, copy-paste package. Goal: your first FLY reel, made 100% free in ~30–45 min.
Tools: **Viggle** (dance) → **CapCut** (finish) → **Buffer/Plann** (schedule). No money needed.

---

## What you'll end up with
One ~8s vertical reel: FLY pulls up fresh, hits a clean dance, throws a peace sign, and
drops one line. You'll export **two versions** (silent + talking) to see which feels right.

---

## STEP 1 — (Optional) Fresh FLY pose in Leonardo/Ideogram
*Skip this if you just want to use your existing duck image — Viggle can animate that directly.*

Upload your duck reference as a **character/image reference**, then prompt:
```
3D chibi vinyl-toy duck, golden-yellow body, big glossy eyes, orange bill, black
backwards snapback cap, tan bomber-hoodie with a "FLY" chest patch, white smiley-face
tee, black shorts, chunky multicolor high-top sneakers. Full body, standing confident,
slight lean, warm smile. Soft Pixar studio lighting, bright Miami-pastel background,
clean, vertical 9:16.
```
Download the PNG. Keep the **full body visible** — Viggle needs the whole character.

---

## STEP 2 — Make him dance (Viggle, free)
1. Go to Viggle's AI Dance tool → upload FLY's image (full body, plain background works best).
2. Pick a **dance template** — choose a **short, clean, bouncy** one (not too fast/complex;
   simple reads better at thumbnail size and loops cleaner). Good vibes > hard choreo.
3. Generate. If the motion looks off, try a different template — it's free.
4. Download the clip. If it has a watermark, re-export the same job in **Hailuo** or
   **Dreamina** (watermark-free free tiers).

> Save the FLY image you used — reuse it every Monday and just swap the dance template.

---

## STEP 3 — Finish in CapCut (free)
1. New project → drop in the Viggle clip.
2. **Trim to ~8s.** Aim for a clean loop (last frame ≈ first frame).
3. **Trending audio:** tap Audio → it flags what's trending. Pick an upbeat one and sync
   the dance to the beat.
4. **On-screen text** (the hook, top-third, big bold):
   - Frame 1 (0–1s): `Monday? We good.`
   - End (6–8s): `Slow down. You're not behind. ✌️`
5. **Watermark:** add a small `FLY ✌️` text/logo bottom-corner, low opacity.
6. **Captions:** CapCut → Auto-captions (only if you do the talking cut).
7. **Export:** 1080×1920, 30fps, high quality. This is your **SILENT cut**. ✅

### Talking cut (the A/B test)
- Duplicate the project.
- CapCut → **Text-to-Speech**: type `Slow down. You're not behind.` → pick a **calm, warm,
  young male** voice. Place it at the end over the peace sign.
- Export again. This is your **TALKING cut**. ✅
