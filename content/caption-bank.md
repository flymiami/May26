# Caption & Hashtag Bank

Copy-paste captions, hooks, CTAs, and hashtag sets. Keep captions short — the video does
the work; the caption sparks a reply.

---

## Caption formula

```
[1 line that adds feeling or a question]
[CTA / engagement bait]
[sign-off ✌️]
.
[hashtags — 5 to 10, mixed sizes]
```

Example:
```
New week, who's bouncing with me? 🕺
Tag your Monday motivation 👇
Stay fly. ✌️
.
#miniflyduck #goodvibes #dancereels #miami #characteranimation
```

---

## On-screen hooks (first frame — the most important text)

- "Monday? We dance. 🕺"
- "POV: the flyest duck in Miami pulls up"
- "Save this for a hard day 💛"
- "Real talk for a sec…"
- "Wait for the landing 👀"
- "You needed to hear this today."
- "Tiny duck. Big advice."
- "Come with FLY to ___"
- "Nobody's talking about this, but…"
- "If you're seeing this, it's your sign ✨"

## CTAs (rotate — don't repeat the same one daily)

- "Follow for a [dance/laugh/reminder] every day."
- "Tag someone who needs this. 👇"
- "Save it for later. 💛"
- "Send this to your [bestie/gym buddy/the homie]."
- "Where should FLY go next? Comment below."
- "Drop a ✌️ if you're with FLY."
- "Follow the journey — new reel daily."

## Sign-offs

- "Stay fly. ✌️"  ·  "— Lil FLY"  ·  "Pass the good energy on. 💛"
