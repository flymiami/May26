## Idea generators (so you never run out)

**Dance (Mon):** trending sounds · slowed/sped versions · "teach FLY this dance" · dance
in different outfits/sneakers · dance with a guest character · transition dances.

**Sport (Tue):** skate, surf, BMX, snowboard, ski, parkour, basketball dunk, soccer
freestyle, rock climbing, motocross, wakeboard, scooter, breakdance-as-sport. Series idea:
**"FLY tries every extreme sport"** — numbered, bingeable.

**Quote (Wed):** confidence · kindness · perseverance · self-worth · dreams · gratitude ·
courage. Format options: text-on-clean-frame · 3-slide carousel · FLY "speaking" it ·
quote revealed by a transition.

**Advice/Help (Thu):** dealing with comparison · burnout · bad days · friendship · trying
again after failure · being kind to yourself. Help-stories: sharing food, including someone
left out, cheering up a friend, standing up for the small guy, random act of kindness.

**Jokes (Fri):** duck/bird puns · sneaker jokes · relatable everyday fails · "expectation
vs reality" · FLY vs. inanimate objects · groan-worthy dad jokes. Keep it clean.

**Lifestyle (Sat):** beach · sneaker shopping · food · "come with me to ___" · trying a
new hobby · Miami spots · get-ready-with-FLY · mini-vlogs.

**Reset (Sun):** gratitude · self-care routine · setting intentions · cozy scenes ·
"wins of the week" · slow living · permission to rest.

## Series ideas (these drive follows hardest)

- **"FLY tries every extreme sport"** — Tuesdays, numbered.
- **"Tiny duck, big advice"** — Thursdays, a recurring format/intro.
- **"FLY's Miami"** — Saturdays, exploring the city.
- **"Quote of the week, but FLY means it"** — Wednesdays, signature look.

> A series gives people a reason to hit follow ("I want the next one") and trains the
> algorithm to show your account as appointment viewing.
