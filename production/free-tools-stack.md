## Make the pilot reel — "The FLY Pull-Up" — for $0

**Step 1 — FLY in the pose (Leonardo or Ideogram).**
- Upload your duck reference as a **character/style reference**.
- Prompt: *"3D chibi vinyl-toy duck, golden-yellow body, big glossy eyes, orange bill,
  black backwards snapback cap, tan bomber-hoodie with 'FLY' chest patch, white smiley
  tee, black shorts, chunky multicolor high-top sneakers. Full body, confident pull-up
  walk toward camera, slight lean, peace sign, warm smile. Soft Pixar lighting, bright
  Miami-pastel background. Vertical 9:16."*
- Download the PNG. *(Or skip this and feed the original duck straight into Step 2.)*

**Step 2 — Make him dance (Viggle AI — the key tool).**
- Upload FLY's image + pick a **dance template** (or describe the move). Viggle maps a
  real dance onto the character = clean, on-beat motion with zero animation skill.
- Alt: **Kling** (upload + "one smooth dance move, then a peace sign, loop-friendly").
- If a tool watermarks, redo the export in **Hailuo** or **Dreamina** (watermark-free free).

**Step 3 — Finish in CapCut (free).**
- Trim to ~8s, make it loop.
- Add on-screen text: **"Slow down. You're not behind. ✌️"** (silent cut).
- Add a **trending audio** (CapCut shows what's trending).
- Drop the **FLY watermark** bottom-corner.
- Talking cut: use CapCut **text-to-speech** for the same line (calm voice) → A/B test.

**Step 4 — Schedule (Buffer / Plann / Adobe Express).**
- Connect Instagram + TikTok, drop the reel on the fixed daily slot. Plann can reshare
  IG Reels → TikTok in one click.

## Smartest free shortcut

Your dance days are basically **free forever** with Viggle: upload FLY once, swap the
dance template each Monday. Quote/advice/Sunday days need only a **still + text in CapCut**
— almost no "credits" at all. The credit-hungry days (full animation) you can ration
across Kling's daily free 66 and Dreamina's daily refresh.
