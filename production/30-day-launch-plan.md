# 30-Day Launch Plan

A day-by-day runway to go from zero to a healthy, habit-formed account in one month.
Pair this with [`content/content-pillars.md`](../content/content-pillars.md) (the 4 weeks
of content) and the [weekly schedule](../content/weekly-schedule.md).

---

## Phase 0 — Setup (before Day 1, ~1 day)

- [ ] Lock the **handle** (`@miniflyduck` recommended) on IG + TikTok + YouTube.
- [ ] Set **display name**, **bio**, profile pic (FLY face lockup), link.
- [ ] Build the **master reference sheet** + style string (see production playbook §0).
- [ ] Create the **cover/thumbnail template** + **watermark** in Adobe Express.
- [ ] Set up **Story Highlights** covers: Dance · Wisdom · Jokes · Kindness · Behind the FLY.
- [ ] Pick the **fixed daily post time** (recommended 6:00 PM ET).
- [ ] **Batch-produce Week 1's 7 reels** before launch so you start ahead.

## Phase 1 — Days 1–7: "Establish the character"

Post Week 1 content ("Meet FLY"). Goals: introduce the duck, set the daily rhythm.

- Day 1: launch with **Move Monday** dance (your strongest reach format) + a pinned
  intro: who FLY is, what to expect (1 reel a day, the weekly menu).
- Every day: post at the fixed time, reply to **every** comment in character within 1 hr.
- Engage outward: comment kindly on 10 mascot/positivity/streetwear accounts daily.
- End of week: note which pillar got the most **watch-time, saves, shares**.

## Phase 2 — Days 8–14: "Find the spark"

Post Week 2 ("FLY Levels Up"). Goals: lean into early signals.

- Double down on whatever format over-performed in Week 1 (give it a little more love).
- Start **one series** (e.g. "FLY tries every extreme sport" on Tuesdays).
- Try one **trend hijack** — ride a climbing sound on Mon or Fri.
- Post your first **carousel quote** (Wed) to test saves.

## Phase 3 — Days 15–21: "Build the world"

Post Week 3 ("FLY Goes Bigger"). Goals: depth + recurring elements.

- Introduce a **recurring setting** (FLY's room, his Miami block).
- Tease a **sidekick** for Week 4.
- Run a **Story poll** ("which sport should FLY try next?") — community co-creates.
- Reach out / collab-comment with 1–2 similar-size creators.

## Phase 4 — Days 22–30: "Deepen the bond"

Post Week 4 ("FLY & Friends"). Goals: community + momentum into Month 2.

- Launch the **sidekick** (Mon) — expands story potential.
- Post a **month recap / thank-you** (Sun) — gratitude builds loyalty.
- Reply to DMs, feature a fan comment in a reel.
- **Review the month** (below) and batch Month 2.

## Weekly review ritual (every Sunday, 15 min)

Track in a simple sheet/Airtable:

| Reel | Pillar | Views | Watch-time | Shares | Saves | Follows |
|------|--------|-------|-----------|--------|-------|---------|

Then ask:
1. Which **pillar/format** is winning? → do more of it.
2. Which **hook styles** held attention? → reuse them.
3. What got **shared/saved**? → that's your growth fuel.
4. Did I keep the **streak**? → protect it above all.

---

## The 5 rules that decide whether this works

1. **Never miss a day.** Consistency beats quality over time (aim for both, but never
   skip). Keep evergreen reels in reserve for busy days.
2. **Stay on-model.** Same duck, every post. Recognition is the whole game.
3. **Ride trends fast.** Trending audio is your biggest free reach. Move within 1–2 days.
4. **Engineer shares & saves.** Quote/advice/joke days should make people hit send/save.
5. **Be the duck in the comments.** Personality in replies turns viewers into a community.

## Month 2+ (where it goes)

- Keep the weekly skeleton; refresh content monthly.
- Grow the **cast** and **recurring series**.
- Test **YouTube Shorts + TikTok** harder if IG is the lead.
- When there's love, explore **merch** (the FLY jacket, smiley tee, stickers, vinyl toy
  via DesignSuites Miami) and **brand collabs**.
