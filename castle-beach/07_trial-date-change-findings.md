## The question
Did Don ever tell the client the **judge** moved / delayed / postponed / changed the
trial date (implying it was out of MFH's hands)?

## Answer: No — and his own emails say the opposite
There is **no** email in which Don attributes the trial change to the judge acting on
his own. In every instance, the change came from a **party motion**, and Don says so:

## Why this matters
1. It contradicts any "the judge delayed it / it was out of my hands" explanation —
   Don's own words show MFH filed the continuances (Dec 2024, June 2025; Jan & Mar 2026
   per the docket), and only one reset (Oct 2025) was the defendant's.
2. It dovetails with the docket: **3 of 4 trial resets were plaintiff/MFH-driven**, two
   of those on motions bearing the client's `/s/` signature (Jan & Mar 2026).
3. The Dec 12, 2024 "we are continuing everything by 30 days … sign the attached"
   email is the same continuance the client later flagged among the "documents signed
   under my name."
