## The governing rule

**Fla. R. Civ. P. 1.460** (amended effective **Jan 1, 2025**): a motion to continue
trial must be in writing and, *except for good cause shown,* **"signed by the named
party requesting the continuance"** — i.e., the client, not the attorney. The
amendment deliberately shifts responsibility to the party so the client personally
knows about and consents to delaying their own trial. Continuances are expressly
**"disfavored"** and granted only for good cause.

## Analysis

1. **The presence of your signature is rule-driven, not unusual in itself.** Because
   1.460 now requires the *party's* signature, every compliant trial-continuance
   motion will bear the client's "/s/". So the mere fact that your name is on it is
   not, by itself, wrongdoing.

2. **The problem is authorization + knowledge.** The "/s/" convention under
   **Fla. R. Jud. Admin. 2.515** represents that the named person *authorized* the
   signature. If MFH typed "s/ Facundo Yebne" without your authorization — and you
   have said you were not told the trial dates were being moved — then each motion
   contained a **false representation to the court that you, the party, requested or
   consented to the continuance.** That is the precise harm Rule 1.460 was amended
   to prevent.

3. **Potential rule violations** (for professional-responsibility counsel to assess,
   not conclusions):
   - **Bar Rule 4-8.4(c)** — conduct involving dishonesty, fraud, deceit, or
     misrepresentation.
   - **Bar Rule 4-3.3** — candor toward the tribunal.
   - **Bar Rule 4-1.4** — failure to keep the client reasonably informed (the four
     resets, none communicated to you).
   - Whether an unauthorized signature in your name rises to **forgery / fraud on
     the court** is a question for professional-responsibility and, if you choose,
     criminal counsel.

4. **It connects to the delay narrative.** These are two of the MFH-driven trial
   resets (Jan 26, 2026 and Mar 31, 2026 orders). Don's June 9 email blames *you*
   for the delays; the motions that caused two of those delays bear *your*
   signature applied by *his* firm.
