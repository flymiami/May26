## Fix before it is final

2. Section numbering. The relief section is labeled "I. DEMAND FOR RELIEF AND ATTORNEY FEES." It should be III (the letter already has I. Factual Background and II. Violations of Florida Law).

3. Page 9 typo. "are themselves part of C14" should read CU14.

4. Page 9 duplicate words. "extends beyond the Condominium's general common areas. areas." Delete the repeated word.

5. Mediator email conflict. Lawrence Saichek is listed with "laslaw18@aol.com" on page 14 and "larry@saicheklaw.com" on page 15. Use one.

6. Entity name. The letter says "Falcon Engineering." The Association's own trial witness list names "The Falcon Group" (witness Milton Perez). Confirm the exact firm name so the horizontal-vs-vertical admission is pinned to the right entity and witness.

7. Name spelling. "Kevin St. Hilaire" on page 6 vs "St. Hillaire" on the Association's witness list. Same person (the inspector who wrote violation notices 156, 158, 162-2025 and is Doyle Security). Make the spelling consistent.

## Two strategy calls

8. Claim-splitting. The Association's 7/27/2026 trial witness list references the Second Amended Complaint in the pending case (2022-003970). Confirm these 2025-2026 construction, fine, and retaliation claims are not already pled there. If they are, filing a separate new suit invites a motion to dismiss for claim-splitting. Amending the pending case may be cleaner. Decide this first.

9. Scope of the mediation. The letter demands only injunctive relief plus property-damage "in an amount to be established upon inspection." It does not mention the valet 50% or the storage lost-profits claims from the pending lawsuit (about $1.88M). Decide whether this mediation is meant to resolve only the new 718 dispute or globally with the pending case, and say so in the letter. If global, put a number in.

## Practical

10. Page 8 embeds a live Google Drive folder link to the Carlos surveillance video and sends it to opposing counsel. Confirm that folder's sharing setting. If it is "anyone with the link," the Association's counsel now has open access to that drive. Consider a controlled production instead.

Note: I am reporting Carlos to the police separately for criminal theft, so keep the letter's wording consistent with whatever the police report says.
