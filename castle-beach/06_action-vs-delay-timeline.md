# Action vs. Delay Timeline — Castle Beach (Dec 2025 – Jun 2026)

**Matter:** Design Suites Miami v. Castle Beach (2022-003970-CA-01).
**Purpose:** Test Don Hayden's claim that he didn't move against the Association /
didn't act because the *client* delayed for months. The email record shows the
opposite: the client produced what was requested promptly and repeatedly pressed
to act; the protective filing and the agency complaints were deferred by counsel
and then never pursued.

## Bottom line
Don's "you delayed for months" does not hold up. You documented the harassment from
December, delivered the affidavit (Mar 30) and the CADISA report (Apr 1), offered to
file the DBPR and agency complaints immediately, and repeatedly chased for action.
The agency complaints awaited his green light that never came; the injunction was
deferred on his own recommendation and then never filed. The delay in acting against
the Association was on counsel's side.
