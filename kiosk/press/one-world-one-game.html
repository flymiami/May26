ONE WORLD. ONE GAME.

Miami artist FLY turns thousands of rubber ducks into a World Cup, a Pride celebration, and a love letter to two nations. Kimpton EPIC Hotel · June 3 to July 27, 2026.

MIAMI, FL. As the world arrives for the 2026 FIFA World Cup, Facundo Yebne, the Miami artist known as FLY, opens ONE WORLD. ONE GAME. at the Kimpton EPIC Hotel.

FLY is the artist behind Miami Beach's 2025 Pride commissions on Lincoln Road, and a Fourth Prize winner for Installation Art at the Florence Biennale XV (2025). For his most ambitious work yet, he fills the hotel's lobby and 16th floor with large scale pop art built entirely from mini rubber and resin ducks.

Three summer moments run through it: the World Cup, Miami Pride, and the 250th anniversary of the United States. The message is unity. Identical little ducks, placed by hand, become images the whole world recognizes.

In the lobby stands UnityBeak Trilogy: Love, a single sculpture of thousands of mini rubber ducks, the first thing every guest meets.

The 16th floor opens onto a right wall built like a World Cup fixture. Groups sit on each side and advance toward the center. As nations move forward, their jerseys are built in ducks and hung, so the wall changes through the tournament, from the USA at one end to Argentina at the other.

USA side, FLY's adopted home and the tournament host: Don't Kuack With Liberty, America Peace, United Kuakies of America, and the Pulisic jersey.

Argentina side, FLY's birthplace and the defending champion: a flag with a glow in the dark center, a heart in yellow, white and light blue, and the Messi jersey. Between the ends hang the flags of Mexico, Japan, Brazil and France, the section that updates as teams advance.

At the center hangs the show's namesake. One World, One Game is a 36 by 36 inch canvas, a circle split in four: the American flag, a glow in the dark soccer ball, the colors of the rainbow, and the South American continent.

Glass doors open to the terrace, where Proud Love Pink stops passers by. The left wall holds a giant Polaroid selfie spot, edged in rubber ducks for guests to sign, beside a peace sign and a smiley in the colors of the Pride flag.

An interactive kiosk tells the story of every work in the artist's own voice. Return during the tournament and the show is different each week.

One material, one message: peace, love, and joy. Soccer is the one language the whole planet speaks, and I build my art the same way, one small duck at a time, until thousands of them become something we all recognize.Facundo Yebne, FLY

About the Artist

Facundo Yebne (FLY) is a Miami based pop artist who builds sculptures and wall works from thousands of rubber and resin ducks. One material, one message: peace, love and joy. In 2025, Miami Beach and Lincoln Road commissioned two public Pride installations: UnityBeak, two giant ducks built from 14,000 mini rubber ducks, and Proud Love, a 10 foot heart built from 7,000 rubber ducks, shown April 2 to July 1, 2025. That same year FLY won Fourth Prize for Installation Art at the Florence Biennale XV. His work has been featured by Time Out, GayCities, Wire Magazine, Artburst Miami and Revista Viagem. This show holds 13 one of a kind originals, each signed with a Certificate of Authenticity, from $1,000 to $10,000 at flymiami.art.
