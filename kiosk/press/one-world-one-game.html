ONE WORLD. ONE GAME.

A FLY Miami exhibition by Facundo Yebne at Kimpton EPIC Hotel, built from thousands of tiny rubber ducks and evolving with the 2026 World Cup. On view now through July 27, 2026.

MIAMI, FL. As the world arrives in Miami for the 2026 FIFA World Cup, Kimpton EPIC Hotel presents ONE WORLD. ONE GAME., a solo exhibition by Miami artist Facundo Yebne, known as FLY. The show spans three floors of the hotel, from the lobby to the 16th floor, with large scale pop art built from thousands of mini rubber and resin ducks, much of it glowing under blacklight.

The exhibition brings together three summer moments: the World Cup, Pride, and the 250th anniversary of the United States. Its message is unity. Thousands of identical little ducks, arranged by hand, become images the whole world recognizes.

The show opens with its two sculptural showpieces. In the lobby, Unitybeak Trilogy: Love greets every guest at the door. On the 16th floor, Pink Proud Love crowns the exhibition's Pride section, a rainbow celebration of love and belonging.

ONE WORLD. ONE GAME. is built to change with the tournament. A growing wall of duck built team jerseys expands in real time as nations advance, with new jerseys added for every team that reaches the semifinals and the final. Return during the World Cup and the show is different each week.

Throughout the space, an interactive kiosk tells the story behind each work, and a Polaroid selfie station invites guests to take a piece of the show home.

Soccer is the one language the whole planet speaks. I build my art the same way, one small duck at a time, until thousands of them become something we all recognize. ONE WORLD. ONE GAME. is my way of saying we are stronger, and a lot more fun, together.Facundo Yebne, FLY

Every piece is a one of a kind original, signed by the artist with a Certificate of Authenticity. Available works can be viewed and acquired at flymiami.art.
