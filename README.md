# Your easy way to work with Claude

You don't need to learn anything. There is **one thing** to remember:

> **Type or say: "Check my inbox."**

That's it. Claude will look across your email, calendar, Slack, and tasks, and
come back with a short numbered list of only the things that actually need *you*.
You reply with the numbers you want handled — or just say **"do it all."**

## What it does for you

When you ask for a rundown, Claude quietly checks everything that's connected and
brings back something like this:

```
☀️ Here's what needs you today — reply with the numbers you want me to do.

REPLY NEEDED
1. Maria — wants to move Thursday's install to Friday.
   → I drafted a "Friday 10am works" reply. [send / edit / skip]

DECIDE
2. New $3,400 wholesale order is waiting to be fulfilled. [mark fulfilled / hold]

SCHEDULE
3. Two things overlap at 2pm Tuesday. [move one / keep both]

HEADS-UP
• An invoice is 12 days overdue.

Just tell me the numbers (e.g. "1 and 3") or say "do it all".
```

You read it in ten seconds. You reply "1 and 2." Claude does it and tells you it's
done. No apps to open, no tabs, nothing to keep up with.

## Things you can say (all plain English)

- "Check my inbox" / "What needs me today?" / "Catch me up."
- "Do it all." / "Just 1 and 3." / "Skip the rest."
- "Show me the details on 2."
- "Do this again at 5pm." (Claude can give you a rundown on a schedule.)

## Where to say it

The same sentence works everywhere — on your **phone**, in the **desktop app**, on
the **web**, or in your **terminal**. Pick whichever is in your hand. You never
have to remember which device you used last; it's the same assistant.

## Good to know

- Claude **shows you a draft and waits for your OK** before sending anything to a
  person or moving any money. Nothing goes out without your say-so.
- If something isn't connected yet (say, your calendar), Claude will mention it in
  one line and keep going — it won't get stuck.
