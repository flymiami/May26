# Character Bible — Mini FLY Duck

The single source of truth for who the duck is and how he looks, moves, and talks.
Consistency here is *the* growth lever — mascot accounts win because the character is
instantly recognizable in a half-second scroll.

---

## 1. Who he is

**Name:** Mini FLY Duck (nickname on-screen: **"FLY"** or **"Lil FLY"**)
**Species:** Pint-sized vinyl-toy duck with a big-city heart.
**Age vibe:** Forever-young, Gen-Z energy. Wise beyond his size.
**Home base:** Miami — sunshine, street culture, color, palm trees, good vibes.
**Essence:** The friend who's always hyped for you. Equal parts hype-man, little
brother, and tiny philosopher in fresh sneakers.

### Personality (the 5 traits)
1. **Joyful** — default state is a big smile and a peace sign. ✌️
2. **Confident, never arrogant** — drip without ego; he gasses *you* up, not himself.
3. **Wholesome** — clean humor, kind heart, helps people, no punching down.
4. **Curious & adventurous** — down to try the dance, the trick, the new thing.
5. **Quietly wise** — drops a real one-liner when you least expect it.

### What he stands for (brand values)
- Good energy is contagious — pass it on.
- Stay fly, stay humble.
- Little guy, big dreams — size is not a limit.
- Help somebody every chance you get.

---

## 2. Visual identity (lock these — never drift)

### The look (from reference art)
- **Body:** bright golden-yellow duck, chibi proportions, big head, big expressive eyes
  with sharp catch-lights, orange bill, permanent warm smile.
- **Signature pose:** peace sign ✌️, slight lean, mid-bounce.
- **Cap:** black backwards snapback (swoosh detail). The cap is iconic — keep it.
- **Jacket:** tan/beige bomber-hoodie with the **"FLY"** patch on the chest + playful
  patches on the sleeve. This is his uniform.
- **Shirt:** white tee with a black smiley face.
- **Bottoms:** black shorts / joggers.
- **Sneakers:** chunky high-top Jordan-1-style kicks in yellow/black/teal/pink/red.
  Sneakers are a personality detail — they can change for special posts.

### Color palette
| Role | Color | Hex |
|------|-------|-----|
| Duck yellow (primary) | Golden yellow | `#FFC21A` |
| Bill / accent orange | Bright orange | `#F47A20` |
| Jacket tan | Warm sand | `#D8B98C` |
| Ink black (cap, text) | Near-black | `#1A1A1A` |
| Pop teal | Mint teal | `#36C9C0` |
| Pop pink | Hot pink | `#FF5C8A` |
| Clean base | Off-white | `#F6F2EA` |

> Use the yellow + black as the brand core; teal/pink/orange as energetic accents
> (sneaker-inspired). High contrast, bright, "Miami toy store" feel.

### Logo / wordmark
- Wordmark: **"FLY"** in a bold, rounded, slightly graffiti-soft display type.
- Lockup: the duck's face + "MINI FLY DUCK" for the profile picture.
- Watermark: small "FLY" tag bottom-corner on every reel (protects content + builds brand).

## 3. Voice & tone

He talks like a hyped, kind friend. Short. Warm. A little slang, never forced.

**Do:** "You got this." · "Stay fly. ✌️" · "Tiny duck, big love." · "Go be great today."
**Don't:** long paragraphs, cynicism, mean jokes, corporate-speak, politics, anything
that punches down.

### Voice by pillar (he code-switches a little)
- **Dancing:** playful, hype — "Pull up. We dancin'."
- **Extreme sport:** brave, adrenaline — "Send it. 🛹"
- **Quotes:** calm, wise — fewer words, more weight.
- **Advice:** warm big-brother — "Real talk for a sec…"
- **Jokes:** goofy, light — groan-worthy puns welcome.
- **Kindness:** sincere, gentle — let the act speak.

### Signature sign-offs (rotate)
- "Stay fly. ✌️"
- "— Lil FLY"
- "Pass the good energy on."
