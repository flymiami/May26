# Amazon sourcing method: Abuv The Par, EEC 2026 (summary)
Source: course Docs in Drive folder "Abuv The Par". Read so far: lessons 1.2, 2.1, 3.2, 4.4, 6.2, 6.9. Numbers below are the course's claims, not tested.

## The model
1. Wholesale plus FBA. Buy established brands in bulk from the brand or its authorized distributors, ship to Amazon, Amazon fulfills.
2. Not private label and not factories. The course calls private label costly and risky for beginners; dropshipping gets banned; arbitrage does not scale.
3. Use FBM only as a backup (Q4 warehouse jams, fast trends).

## Step 1. Build a brand lead list
1. Tools: SmartScout, Keepa, DS QuickView, ASINZen (AMZScout free).
2. Methods: SmartScout brand search, reverse sourcing, leaf sourcing, storefront stalking, Google Maps distributors, trade shows.
3. Only authorized sources. Keep a paper trail (invoices) to survive authenticity complaints.

## Step 2. Pick the right products
1. 3 or more FBA sellers on the listing (brand allows resellers).
2. Best Seller Rank 1 to about 100K (200K max).
3. Keepa seller count stable; a sudden drop to 1 seller means the brand files complaints. Avoid.
4. Avoid listings where Amazon itself sells.

## Step 3. Outreach
1. Big brands (over about $300K/month): short email plus call to customer service or sales asking who their distributors are. Course says about 10% reply, often several distributors each.
2. Small brands: go direct, lead with free value (Loom video on their listings and competitors), target executives, follow up 3 to 6 months. Course says 2 to 3% reply.
3. Course suggests beginners start with path 1.

## Step 4. Profit check before buying
1. Use the LOWEST stable Keepa price of the last 6 to 9 months (held 2 weeks or more), not today's price.
2. Add prep (about $0.50 to $0.65 per unit) and inbound shipping ($0.05 to $1 per unit).
3. Example floor used in the course: at least $2 net profit and 12% net margin per unit.
4. ROI over about 120% usually means a multipack mismatch. Check the real listing.
5. Units you can sell per month = average monthly sales of 2 tools / (FBA sellers + 1). Variation listings need the per variant number.
6. Work back to the max price you can pay the supplier.

## Step 5. Buy small, then scale
1. Test order first, negotiate after it sells, then place professional POs (course templates in modules 4 and 7).
2. Use a prep center (questions list in module 7).

## What this means for you (to confirm)
1. Your Amazon store today sells your own products (translation earbuds, turkey hats). That is closer to private label than to this course.
2. Applying the course means adding a wholesale line: branded products bought from distributors. It needs cash for POs, which is tight now.
