## Answers

### 1. Buyer offers 30% under asking for a sculpture (Nov 14 2026)
Facundo: "It depends on the client and the artwork. If it's a new piece and it's unfinished, I would say, okay, but I don't put the frame or I don't do this or do that, because if you're paying less, then you should get less or something like that. It depends also on my need of cash, but I would say if they want a discount, either remove part of the artwork that they are trying to get a discount, or bundle it for more money with another one."
Rule: never give a straight discount. A lower price means less work (no frame, fewer elements) or a bigger deal (bundle with another piece). Flex depends on the client, the piece, and how much cash is needed right now.
