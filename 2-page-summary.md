## Page 1 — The Opportunity

**The thesis.** Build the **Pop Mart × KAWS × Supreme of the rubber duck**: one owned character IP extended across **art, collectible ducks, apparel/merch, and creative workshops**. This is not a product company — it's a **character-IP holding company**. The moat is *owning the IP*, the single lever that separates Pop Mart (owned IP, ~67% gross margin, historically 30x+ P/E) from Funko (licensee, sub-1x revenue valuation).

**Why a duck.** The most universally loved, baggage-free icon on earth — and it *already* shows organic collector behavior: the viral "Duck-Duck-Jeep" gifting movement (millions of Jeep owners), and CelebriDucks' premium artisan ducks at ~$17–20. The niche is real but **fragmented — no design-led IP brand owns it yet.** That's the white space.

**Why now (tailwinds):**
- **Kidults drive the market** — adults were **28% of global toy sales in 2024** (Circana) and are the cohort stabilizing the entire $123B toy industry.
- **Blind-box scarcity is a proven engine** — the mechanic powers ~61% of Pop Mart's revenue; "chase" variants create resale premiums (Labubu averaged **+23%** on StockX, H1 2025).
- **The artist-empire playbook is mainstream** — KAWS, Murakami, Kusama, Arsham, MSCHF all ran the same flywheel.
- **Miami = a free annual global stage** — Art Basel Miami Beach 2024: **~75,000 attendees, ~$547M economic impact.**

- **SAM:** multi-billion-dollar serviceable slice (kidult designer toys + character-IP apparel + accessible art + workshops).
- **SOM (3–5 yr):** a focused artist-IP brand can credibly reach **$5–25M annual revenue** pre-breakout. Reference: MSCHF raised at a **$200M valuation**; Chamberlain Coffee hit ~$20M; Pop Mart is the (extreme) upside — one character → ~$1.8B revenue.

**Comps double as the roadmap.** Pop Mart (own IP → premium multiple), KAWS/Murakami (art credibility → mass collabs), Supreme (drop/scarcity → $2.1B), Stance/MeUndies ($75–95M on socks/underwear alone). **Cautionary comp: Funko** — ~$1B revenue but ~$243M market cap, because it *licenses* others' IP. **Lesson: own the character.**

## Page 2 — The Business & The Ask

**The conglomerate flywheel.** Art builds credibility → Collectibles monetize it at high margin via scarcity → Apparel/merch convert fans at accessible prices and act as walking ads → Workshops turn buyers into a repeat-buying community that generates content → all four make the character more famous, lifting the value of the art. *Repeat.* No single SKU is load-bearing.

**Revenue streams & margins:**

| Stream | Price band | Gross margin (benchmark) |
|---|---|---|
| Collectible ducks (blind box / drops) | $15–150 (secondary $500–5,000) | 50–67% (target between Funko ~40% & Pop Mart ~67%) |
| Apparel & merch (tees, socks, underwear) | $15–60 | 50–65% DTC (POD 30–50%, no inventory) |
| Art & limited editions | $50–5,000+ | high / 10–20% if licensed |
| Workshops & experiences | $35–150/session | high-margin services |
| Licensing (later) | royalty | 5–20% of wholesale/retail |

**Illustrative 3-year ramp** *(directional assumptions, not forecasts):*

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Revenue** | ~$1.0M | ~$3.7M | ~$9.4M |
| Blended gross margin | 48% | 53% | 58% |
| **EBITDA** | (~$0.4M) | (~$0.1M) | **~$1.0M** |

At ~$1M EBITDA and consumer-products multiples (~6.8–9× EBITDA), that frames a **~$7–9M+ enterprise value on fundamentals alone** — before any IP/brand-breakout premium.

**Top risks → mitigations:** collectible hype is cyclical (Pop Mart fell ~50% in 2025) → build a *system* + a *cast of characters*, not a one-trend bet; over-dropping erodes scarcity (Supreme) → drop discipline; the licensee trap → **own the IP, license out later.**

### The Ask — recommended phased raise

You mentioned a single "Shark Tank"-style investor. The honest math: on-air deals average **~$286K for ~24.7% equity** — giving away a quarter of the very asset you want to own. So we recommend a phased path that proves demand and *raises* your valuation before dilution:

1. **Phase 0 — Crowdfund the flagship duck drop** (~$40–80K of *demand*, zero dilution). Pre-sells inventory, proves demand, builds the list. *Highest-leverage first move.*
2. **Phase 1 — ~$500K pre-seed SAFE** at a **~$4–6M post-money cap**. *This is where a celebrity/strategic angel adds real value* — for distribution + halo, on clean SAFE terms, not a 25% equity grab.
3. **Phase 2 — Seed ($1.5–3M)** once **LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1, CAC payback < 12 mo, strong repeat rate, and a breakout hero SKU** are proven — at 3–5× the Phase-1 valuation.

**Use of ~$500K pre-seed:** ~35% inventory & production · ~20% brand/content/community · ~15% Miami flagship + workshops · ~15% disciplined marketing · ~10% key hires · ~5% **legal/IP (trademark the characters — the asset itself).**

We're doing that with the most universally loved icon on earth — the duck — across art, apparel, and workshops, launched from the world's biggest art stage in Miami.
